## 🚀 Scope of Work

### 1. Billing & POS Software Setup
Deployed a complete Billing / POS system covering dine-in order management, thali & à la carte pricing structures, and Zomato/Swiggy-aligned pricing formulas — see `/docs/menu-pricing/`.

### 2. CCTV & Security Systems
Planned and coordinated installation of CCTV surveillance systems across the dining and kitchen areas for operational safety and vigilance.

### 3. Vigilance, Paperwork & Administration
- Complete administrative paperwork handling for branch registration.
- Regulatory and licensing documentation support.
- Operational vigilance framework for day-to-day compliance monitoring.

### 4. Zomato & Swiggy Onboarding
Managed full registration and go-live process on **Zomato** and **Swiggy** delivery portals, including menu structuring, pricing formulas, and commission-adjusted rate cards — see `/docs/menu-pricing/Mahdev_Thali_Restaurant_Pricing-Zomato-Formula.xlsx` and `/docs/menu-pricing/Zomato-Mahadev-Restarurant-Menu-Sub-page-dining-pricing.xlsx`.

### 5. Vendor Alignment (Frozen, Fresh & Restaurant Supply)
Established and coordinated relationships with frozen goods, fresh produce, and general restaurant supply vendors, aligning delivery schedules with vendor **billing cycles** to streamline procurement and cash flow.

### 6. Social Media Handle Management
Set up and managed the restaurant's social media presence (Instagram, Facebook, and related handles) for complete brand promotion, local discovery, and customer engagement — powered by MetroMate's performance marketing playbook.

### 7. Operations Documentation
Delivered structured operational documents including staff timing schedules (English & Hindi), full dining menu with sub-page pricing, and a complete operations manual — see `/docs/operations/`.
